About Us

A student-run competitive cybersecurity team.

Our Mission

We compete in Capture The Flag competitions worldwide to sharpen our offensive and defensive security skills. Beyond competing, we run workshops, share knowledge through writeups, and grow the local cybersecurity community.

What We Do

From web exploitation and binary pwn to cryptanalysis and forensics, our team covers the full CTF spectrum. We also build open-source tools, mentor beginners, and participate in outreach events like Start2School.
