> **Come find us outside LT2A to learn more, meet the team, and get started in CTFs!**

## NTU CCDS CTF Team — NUT Flaggers

**Official NTU College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS) CTF Competition Team**

### Vision and Mission
- To grow a close-knit community of CTFers who learn, compete, and improve together
- Dedicated team of CTF players focused on building strong technical foundations across all CTF categories
- Representing NTU CCDS at inter-varsity and national CTF competitions

---

### Past Experiences

- **SANS Spark CTF 2026** (7th Place overall)
  - Competed with local JCs, Polytechnics, and Universities in a competitive on-site environment
  - First-ever on-site CTF competition for NUT Flaggers
- **DSTA’s Cybersecurity Defenders Discovery Camp 2026 (CDDC)** (19th Place, Uni category)
  - Competed with local Pre-universities and Universities in a competitive on-site environment
  - Fielded 4 teams to participate competitively in the CTF back in June 2026

---

### What You Can Look Forward To

- **Hands-on, category-based training** so you can focus on the categories you are best in
- **Community built on respect** for everyone’s strengths so we can work effectively together
- **Mentorship from experienced players** so you can learn from the best
- **Representing NTU CCDS** in international/national CTF competitions
- **Weekly online CTF meetups** to practice
- **Bi-weekly meetings** to learn from everyone’s progress and keep up-to-date with latest CTF happenings
